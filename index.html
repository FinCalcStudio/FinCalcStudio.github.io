06Good boundaries

A few clear answers.

The shortest path to trust is saying what the product does — and what it deliberately does not.

What does FinCalc calculate?+

General financial mathematics: regular payments, payment schedules, equal-period cash flows (NPV, IRR, MIRR) and explicit rate conversions.

Is it a loan quote or compliance calculator?+

No. Results are based on entered values and selected mathematical conventions. Fees, taxes, insurance and product-specific or country rules are not included.

Where is my data stored?+

The core experience is local and works offline. FinCalc has no account, no ads and no default cloud sync. Export and sharing are explicit user actions.

What does Full Access unlock?+

A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited history, scenarios and comparison, plus complete text / CSV export and future general mathematics modules.

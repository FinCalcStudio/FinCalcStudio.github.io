FinCalc Studio · Privacy

Your calculations stay on your device by default.

FinCalc is a local-first financial mathematics workspace for iPhone and iPad. This page explains what the app stores, what Apple processes for purchases, and what happens when you export a record.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Privacy summary

FinCalc does not require a FinCalc account. Core calculations and saved history work offline and are stored locally in the app's Application Support data. FinCalc has no advertising and does not send financial inputs to a FinCalc analytics service.

Information stored on your device

  • Calculation inputs, scenarios, history records, selected conventions, and results are stored locally so the app can work offline.
  • Preferences such as the selected app language are stored locally to keep the interface consistent between launches.
  • FinCalc has no FinCalc account, default cloud sync, advertising SDK, or financial-input analytics.

In-App Purchases

Apple processes payment, purchase, and restore transactions through StoreKit and the App Store. FinCalc does not receive or store your payment card details. The app uses Apple's transaction result to determine whether Full Access is unlocked.

For billing questions, refunds, or an App Store transaction problem, use Apple's purchase support. For an app issue, use the FinCalc support page.

Exports and sharing

Text and CSV exports are created only when you choose an export or share action. An export can contain the financial inputs, assumptions, conventions, and results that you entered. Review the file before sharing it with another person or app.

Once you send a file to another app, service, or recipient, that destination's privacy policy applies to the copy it receives.

Deleting your data

Use FinCalc's history or workspace controls to delete saved calculation records. You can also remove exported text or CSV files from the location where you saved or shared them. Deleting the app removes its local container according to iOS or iPadOS behavior; copies made outside the app are managed by the destination where you placed them.

This website and support requests

This static website does not ask for a FinCalc account and has no FinCalc advertising or financial-input form. If you choose the GitHub support-request link, GitHub may require an account and its own privacy policy applies to the information you submit there. Please do not include bank details or other sensitive financial information in a support request.

Questions or corrections?

Read the FinCalc Support page for troubleshooting and purchase-restore steps. If this privacy information does not answer your question, use the support request link there.
